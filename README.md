## Why Wicaby

- **One app for Emby and Jellyfin**, even several servers at once, on every device.
- **Make it yours**: seven themes (Graphite, OLED, Slate, Cinema, High Contrast, and Light and Automatic on desktop and phones), eight accent colours, and the wallpaper's colour on Android 12 and later phones.
- **The original file, whenever possible.** Wicaby plays your files directly and only asks the server to convert when the device truly can't.
- **HDR and Dolby Vision done right**, with automatic Windows HDR switching and surround passthrough to your receiver.
- **Music done properly**: gapless, ReplayGain and bit-perfect output on desktop, a queue you can edit, mixes, lyrics, and music that keeps playing on the TV. Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers too, with their stars, playlists and scrobbling.
- **Plex too**: link your Plex account with a code, and your Plex servers, and the ones shared with you, sit next to Emby and Jellyfin. An unofficial client, not affiliated with Plex.
- **Your files too**: folders, WebDAV and SMB shares, browsed and played as they are, next to your servers.
- **Real Live TV**: a full guide, quick zapping, a mini-guide and recordings, on every screen. No tuner on your server? Add an IPTV channel list (M3U) with its XMLTV guide.
- **Your server from anywhere**, with Tailscale and NetBird built in. No VPN app to install.
- **Cast to any screen**: "Play on" another Wicaby, Emby or Jellyfin app, a DLNA TV or a Chromecast, from one Cast button. AirPlay is not supported.
- **Private by design**: no tracking, no analytics, no crash reports.

## Features

**Everywhere**
- Continue Watching, Up Next, search, favorites and collections.
- Skip intros, recaps and credits (from the server, or optionally from AniSkip, TheIntroDB and IntroDB community timestamps), autoplay of the next episode, sleep timer.
- Bonus features on detail pages; mark a season or all earlier episodes as watched.
- Pick versions, audio and subtitles; Wicaby remembers your choices per title.
- Find and download subtitles from inside the player.
- Live TV with guide, recordings and reminders, always in the menu, with a setup page when no server has a tuner.
- IPTV: an M3U or M3U8 channel list (Xtream-style addresses too) and an XMLTV guide, played through the Live TV pages, with no server involved.
- Comic reader, synced to your server: CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT, PDF and image EPUB books.
- Photos: a timeline of your photo and home-video libraries, albums, favorites, a viewer with capture details, slideshows and "On This Day".
- Music: albums, artists, songs, genres and playlists, an editable queue, mix and radio, synced lyrics from your server, and ReplayGain.
- File sources: play videos from a folder, a WebDAV server or an SMB share, with NFO titles, posters, sidecar subtitles and progress kept on the device.
- Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers: your music with Navidrome's stars and playlists, lyrics, and plays scrobbled to the server; choose which server the Music section shows ([details](docs/features.md#navidrome)).
- Plex servers: link with a code, Plex Home profiles with their PIN, browsing, playback and resume, and Plex's own Home rows ([what works](docs/features.md#plex)).
- Themes: Graphite (the default), OLED, Slate, Cinema and High Contrast, with eight accent colours.
- Watch history.
- Every Wicaby app can be driven with "Play on" from another Wicaby, Emby or Jellyfin app.
- A short welcome wizard on first launch (language, look, your server, remote access), and a Remote access page with diagnostics you can share.
- English and French.

**Windows**
- Smooth playback with hardware decoding, refresh-rate matching and HDR output, and an optional Smooth Playback mode that times frames to the display.
- Surround passthrough to your receiver (Dolby, DTS, TrueHD).
- Server administration without the web dashboard.
- An optional photo map (OpenStreetMap), off until you turn it on.
- Offline downloads and qBittorrent control.
- Media keys, taskbar controls, Mica, Light and Automatic themes, gamepad support.
- An update notice when a new version is out, and a GitHub button in the title bar that turns into Update. Nothing installs by itself.
- A play queue with shuffle, plus Upcoming and "Because you watched" rows.
- Continue Watching from the jump list and the tray.
- Watch history and statistics, Trakt sign-in and scrobbling.
- Admin alerts and parental access schedules.
- One Cast button: "Play on" another app, a DLNA TV or player, or a Chromecast or Google TV, with a remote while it plays.

**Linux**
- The Windows app's features, playing through your distribution's mpv: VA-API, NVDEC or Vulkan decoding, and PipeWire, PulseAudio or ALSA output with passthrough.
- Media keys and desktop media controls (MPRIS), system tray, launch at login and notifications.
- Refresh-rate matching on X11 and on KDE Plasma (Wayland).
- SDR output: HDR titles are tone-mapped.

**Android TV**
- A remote-first interface with a hero carousel and screensaver.
- A screensaver that can show your photos, one album or "On This Day" instead of film artwork.
- HDMI audio passthrough and display mode matching.
- Paused titles in the launcher's "Continue watching" row.
- Remote access sign-in with a QR code.
- Voice search, an external player as a fallback, the system screensaver and launcher channels (Next Up, Recently Added).
- Sign in with your phone: scan the code on the TV. Then "Play on" from the phone or any Emby or Jellyfin app.
- Night mode, dialogue enhancement and volume boost; styled subtitles; themes and accent colors.
- Shuffle and a play queue, "Add to Playlist" and "Add to Collection", customizable home rows.
- Several servers at once, watch history and statistics, Trakt sign-in and scrobbling.
- "Who's Watching?" profiles with a PIN, parental access schedules, and voice search for Live TV channels and programmes.

**Phones and tablets**
- Gestures for seeking, volume, brightness and picture format.
- Picture-in-Picture and background audio.
- Offline downloads that sync your progress when you're back online, and an offline home when the server can't be reached.
- Several servers at once, themes including Light and Automatic, and the wallpaper's accent colour on Android 12 and later.
- Voice search and an external player for files the phone can't play.
- "Play on" your TV or another app, or cast to a DLNA TV; the Cast build adds Chromecast and Google TV. Connect a TV by photographing its code.

The complete list, including privacy details, is in [docs/features.md](docs/features.md).

## Download

Get the files from the [latest release](https://github.com/WicaebethTheo/wicaby/releases/latest). The desktop app tells you when a new version is out, and the Android apps offer updates from inside the app.

**Windows 10 or 11 (x64)**

| File | |
|---|---|
| `Wicaby-<version>-x64.exe` | **Installer** (recommended) |
| `Wicaby-<version>-portable.exe` | **Portable**, no installation |

**Linux (x64)**: needs **mpv 0.37 or later** from your distribution (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04, Fedora 40 or later ship it).

| File | |
|---|---|
| `Wicaby-<version>-amd64.deb` | **Debian and Ubuntu** (recommended): `sudo apt install ./Wicaby-<version>-amd64.deb` installs mpv with it |
| `Wicaby-<version>-x86_64.AppImage` | **Any distribution**: `chmod +x`, then run it; install mpv first |
| `Wicaby-<version>-x64.tar.gz` | **Any distribution**, unpacked where you like; install mpv first |

Known limits on Linux: video is SDR only, and in a Wayland session mpv plays in its own window with its own controls (mpv's on-screen controller and keys); refresh-rate matching needs X11 or KDE Plasma; without a system keyring (GNOME Keyring, KWallet) sign-ins last until Wicaby closes unless you choose to keep them unencrypted; the tray icon needs a desktop that shows one (on GNOME, the AppIndicator extension). Updates are not installed for you: Wicaby says when one is out, then install the new package.

Linux environment variables: `WICABY_OZONE=x11` runs Wicaby under XWayland in a Wayland session, so mpv plays inside Wicaby's player window again (`wayland`, the default in a Wayland session, or `auto`); mpv can only embed into an X11 window. `WICABY_DISABLE_GPU=1` turns off GPU acceleration of the interface, for GPU process crashes (seen on hybrid Intel + NVIDIA laptops); mpv keeps its own hardware decoding.

**Android TV** (Android 6.0 or later): `Wicaby-TV-foss-<version>-<abi>.apk`

| APK | Pick it for |
|---|---|
| `armv7` | **Most TVs and streaming sticks**, Chromecast with Google TV and Fire TV sticks |
| `arm64` | NVIDIA Shield and other 64-bit boxes |
| `universal` | When you're not sure (larger download) |

**Phones and tablets** (Android 8.0 or later): `Wicaby-Mobile-foss-<version>-<abi>.apk`, `arm64` for most phones, `armv7` for older ones. `Wicaby-Mobile-gplay-<version>-<abi>.apk` adds Chromecast and Google TV through Google Play services (proprietary, see [LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md](LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md)) and has no FFmpeg audio decoder; each one updates only to its own kind.

Installing on a TV and turning on automatic updates: see [Install on a TV](apps/android/README.md#install-on-a-tv) and [Updates for sideloaded APKs](apps/android/README.md#updates-for-sideloaded-apks).

**iPhone and iPad** (iOS 17.1 or later): the phone web app, installed from Safari, nothing to download or sign. Open [wicaebeththeo.github.io/wicaby](https://wicaebeththeo.github.io/wicaby/) in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**: it opens full screen like an app and updates itself. Emby and Jellyfin over HTTPS; MKV files play without conversion (remuxed on the phone, Dolby Vision, Dolby Digital Plus and Blu-ray subtitles included), with downloads, AirPlay and administration. See [docs/mobile-web.md](docs/mobile-web.md).

**LG and Samsung TVs (test builds)**: the TV web app, the Android TV app rebuilt for Samsung Tizen 4+ and LG webOS 4+ (sets from 2018 on), with Emby and Jellyfin: the player (AVPlay on Tizen), Live TV, music, photos, comics, several servers and Trakt. Used on a Samsung Q60T (Tizen 5.5); not yet tried on a real LG set. It is not part of any release yet: the unsigned `.wgt` and the `.ipk` are workflow artifacts. See [docs/tv-web.md](docs/tv-web.md).

## Getting started

1. **Install** Wicaby for your device (see [Download](#download)).
2. **Add your server.** A short welcome wizard guides you: Wicaby finds Emby and Jellyfin servers on your network, or type the address. Plex links with a code, and Navidrome, IPTV lists and file shares are added next to your servers. Away from home? Sign in to Tailscale or NetBird right in the app ([guide](docs/remote-access.md)).
3. **Enjoy.** Sign in with your server account and press play.

## For developers

Requires Windows 10/11, Node.js 24 and Go; the Android apps also need JDK 17+ and the Android SDK.

```powershell
npm install
npm run fetch-mpv                         # pinned mpv build into vendor/mpv
powershell -File apps/desktop/scripts/fetch-mpv.ps1 -Generic   # instead, for CPUs without AVX2
npm run build:mesh                        # the remote-access helper
npm run dev                               # desktop app with hot reload
npm run checks                            # self-checks
cd apps/android; ./gradlew :tv:assembleDebug :mobile:assembleDebug
```

No server at hand? `node tools/mock-server/mock-emby.mjs 8104` starts a mock (user `Wika`, password `wicaby`).
